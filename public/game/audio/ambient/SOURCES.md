# Ambient audio sources

The files in this folder were supplied by the project owner from OpenGameArt and Pixabay downloads. Preserve the original filenames so the source can be traced.

- `bat_01.flac`, `bat_03.flac` — OpenGameArt, “Bat Screeches”, CC0.
- `killconfirmed.wav` — OpenGameArt Electricity Game Sound Pack, CC0.
- `magiazzz-crickets-553217.mp3` — Pixabay Content License.
- `schorsch1964-night-atmosphere-with-crickets-374652.mp3` — Pixabay Content License.
- `freesound_community-rain-drops-and-crickets-in-the-beautiful-state-of-veracruz-mexico-17323.mp3` — Pixabay Content License.
- `dragon-studio-distant-dog-barking-472373.mp3` — Pixabay Content License.
- `dbsound-dog-barking-in-distant-502553.mp3`, `dbsound-dog-barking-in-distant-502559.mp3` — Pixabay Content License.
- `freesound_community-night-ambience-17064.mp3` — Pixabay Content License.
- `alex_jauk-supermarket-ambience-sounds-518582.mp3` — Pixabay Content License.

Runtime selection:

- City bed: `freesound_community-night-ambience-17064.mp3` at low volume.
- Cricket bed: `schorsch1964-night-atmosphere-with-crickets-374652.mp3` at very low volume.
- Bats: randomized one-shots from `bat_01.flac` and `bat_03.flac`.
- Dogs: the short Dragon Studio bark is common; the 18-second dbsound recording is rare and quiet.
- Shop: `alex_jauk-supermarket-ambience-sounds-518582.mp3` looped at low volume.
- Power return: `killconfirmed.wav`, once and quiet on a city win.

Intentionally unused in the current mix:

- Rain/crickets recording: there is no rain visual state in the game.
- Five-second cricket clip: the longer cricket bed already supplies this layer.
- 79-second dog recording: too dominant for a sparse urban-night mix.
